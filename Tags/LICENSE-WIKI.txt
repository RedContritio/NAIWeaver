The descriptions in `wiki-descriptions.json` are derived from the source
above. They have been:
  - filtered to tags present in this app's tag library
  - converted from DText markup to simplified markdown by
    `Tags/dtext_to_markdown.py`
  - deduplicated and trimmed of stub entries (<20 chars)
